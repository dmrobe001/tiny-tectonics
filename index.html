Phase 1–2 of the design notes: plate-frame crust, compositing with subduction polarity, arcs, erosion, a spherical-harmonic mantle, hotspots, and a torque balance (basal drag, slab pull, contact resistance) that sets every plate's spin each substep. Cratons are thick mantle-lithosphere roots seeded at the start; thick crust founders. Every 10 Myr, plates stalled against each other weld, each plate tests its weakest lines for rifting, and old passive margins may start subducting. Known issue: by about 1.6 Gyr most runs jam into a few slow plates.
